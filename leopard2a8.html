01 / THE PLATFORM

BUILT ON A
PROVEN LINE.

The A8 continues the Leopard 2’s development rather than replacing its core layout. It retains the four-person crew and the family’s 120 mm smoothbore cannon, while adding a modernized suite of protection and digital systems.

Unlike a simple field retrofit, the A8 is offered as a new-build configuration. Equipment and details can differ between national orders, so published figures should be read as a guide, not a universal specification.

02 / DESIGN NOTES

AN EVOLUTION
IN PROTECTION.

The A8 combines upgraded protection with current-generation electronics, while keeping the familiar Leopard 2 crew arrangement and operating concept.

01 / ACTIVE PROTECTION01

TROPHY APS

The A8 configuration includes Trophy active protection. It adds a sensor-based defensive layer designed to detect and counter certain incoming threats; it complements rather than replaces armour.

03 / ARMAMENT & DEFENCE

THE 120 MM
AT THE CORE.

The A8 pairs the Leopard 2 family’s main gun with machine-gun armament and layered defensive systems. Exact weapon fit and ammunition depend on the operator.

PRIMARY / 01120 MM

SMOOTHBORE CANNON

A Rheinmetall 120 mm smoothbore cannon is the principal weapon. Public Dutch procurement material describes programmable ammunition as part of its A8 configuration.

04 / IN CONTEXT
A CONTINUING PLATFORM

FAMILIAR SHAPE.
NEW SYSTEMS.

The Leopard 2A8 develops a design that has served since 1979. Its four-person crew and 120 mm main gun remain recognizable, but contemporary sensors, communications, and active protection are central to the new configuration.

Procurement plans are specific to each country. In 2025, the Netherlands announced an order for 46 Leopard 2A8 tanks, with deliveries then expected to begin in 2028.
