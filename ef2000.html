01 / THE RADAR

A new way
to look ahead.

AESA stands for Active Electronically Scanned Array—a radar architecture designed to steer its beam electronically, without moving the antenna.

On the Typhoon, the ECRS (European Common Radar System) family represents an ongoing evolution of the aircraft’s sensor suite. Different configurations and capabilities are introduced across partner nations and aircraft upgrades.

More than a radar.

01

Electronic agility

Rapid beam steering enables the radar to revisit areas of interest and manage multiple tasks with greater flexibility.

02

Radar is one part of a broader sensor suite, working alongside other systems to support the pilot’s situational awareness.

03

Designed to evolve

The ECRS roadmap reflects continuing development, with capabilities shaped by the needs of participating nations.

03 / MISSION LOADOUT

Flexible by design.
Configured for the mission.

Typhoon can carry weapons for air-to-air and air-to-surface missions. This overview covers the weapons named here; it is not a universal or exhaustive loadout. Fit depends on operator, aircraft standard, and approved integration.
