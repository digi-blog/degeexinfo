01 / THE PLATFORM

EVOLVED FOR
THE PRESENT.

Introduced into Bundeswehr service in 2021, the Leopard 2A7V represents a substantial upgrade to Germany’s Leopard 2 fleet. It combines refinements to protection, crew systems, and mobility while retaining the familiar four-person crew and the Leopard 2’s established layout.

The “V” is commonly explained as verbessert—“improved.” Rather than a clean-sheet vehicle, the A7V is a carefully developed chapter in a long-running platform story.

04 / IN CONTEXT
A FAMILY OF UPGRADES

ONE NAME.
MANY GENERATIONS.

Since the Leopard 2 entered service in 1979, successive variants have responded to changing requirements and new technology. The A7V continues that incremental approach: update the platform, preserve what works, and keep the vehicle adaptable.

Its designation also nods to history: “A7V” recalls Germany’s First World War tank, while the modern vehicle belongs to an entirely different era and design lineage.
